02 — Privacy

Privacy Policy

Toss-Up is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • No personal data is collected or transmitted.
  • No account or sign-in required.
  • No third-party tracking or analytics.
  • Flip history is stored locally on your device only.
  • Haptic feedback uses only on-device hardware — no data is sent.
  • Ad functionality (if shown) is handled by Google AdMob in accordance with their privacy policy.
